Ask about the Land Use Act, C of O, Governor's Consent, SURCON, survey plans, and how to buy land safely.

Land Use Act 1978 — Key Provisions

Cap L5 LFN 2004 · Entrenched in Section 315 of the 1999 Constitution

The Single Most Important Rule:

"All land comprised in the territory of each State is vested in the Governor of that State and shall be held in trust and administered for the use and common benefit of all Nigerians." — LUA 1978, Section 1

SectionPlain Meaning
Sec. 1 — Land vested in GovernorThe State holds all land. You own a right of occupancy, not the land itself.
Sec. 5 — Statutory Right of OccupancyGovernor grants SRO for urban land. Valid for up to 99 years.
Sec. 6 — Customary Right of OccupancyLocal Government grants CRO for rural/agricultural land.
Sec. 9 — Certificate of OccupancyThe official document proving your right of occupancy.
Sec. 22 — Governor's Consent requiredAll sales, transfers, mortgages, and leases MUST have Governor's approval.
Sec. 26 — Void without consentAny land transaction without Governor's Consent is legally null and void.
Sec. 28 — Revocation of rightsGovernment can revoke land for "overriding public interest."
Sec. 29 — CompensationOn revocation, you get paid for buildings/improvements only — not the land value.
📋

Nigerian Land Documents — Hierarchy

🥇 Certificate of Occupancy (C of O)

The gold standard. Issued by the Governor. Valid for 99 years. Strongest form of title in Nigeria.

📄 Deed of Assignment

Transfers seller's right of occupancy to buyer. Must be registered and Governor's Consent obtained after the transfer.

🗺️ Registered Survey Plan

Required for all title documents. Must be prepared by a SURCON-registered surveyor and approved/stamped a Registered Surveyor.

✅ Governor's Consent Letter

Formal government approval for any land transaction. Without it, the transaction is void (Sec. 26, LUA 1978).

🏦 Deed of Mortgage

Used when land is collateral for a loan. Also requires Governor's Consent.

SURCON — Surveyors Council of Nigeria

Act No. S18 LFN 2004 · surcon.gov.ng · 10 Yawuri Street, Garki 2, Abuja

What SURCON Does

  • • Regulates the surveying profession in Nigeria
  • • Maintains the public Register of Surveyors
  • • Sets professional standards
  • • Investigates complaints (Surveyors Investigating Panel)
  • • Takes disciplinary action against unregistered practice

Practising Without Registration

  • • Criminal offence under SURCON Act
  • • Survey plan is legally invalid
  • • Registered Surveyor won't approve the plan
  • • Cannot process a C of O
  • • Report to surcon.gov.ng

SURCON Registration Path:

  1. 1Complete B.Sc/B.Tech in Surveying & Geoinformatics from an accredited institution
  2. 2Enroll as a Pupil Surveyor with SURCON (1–2 years supervised internship)
  3. 3Sit and pass the SURCON Professional Examination
  4. 4Attend the SURCON Induction Ceremony (oath-taking)
  5. 5Name added to SURCON Register — now a Registered Surveyor (R.Surv.)
  6. 6Renew registration annually
🔍

How to Verify a Registered Surveyor

✅ Signs of a Legitimate Surveyor

  • ✓Has a unique SURCON registration number (e.g. S2023/XXX)
  • ✓Uses "R.Surv." after their name
  • ✓Survey plans carry their seal, signature, SURCON number, and Registered Surveyor's approval stamp
  • ✓Plans include a beaconing certificate
  • ✓Has a physical office address
  • ✓Name verifiable on surcon.gov.ng

🚩 Red Flags — Walk Away

  • ✗No SURCON registration number on documents
  • ✗Cannot produce a registration certificate
  • ✗Survey plan has no Registered Surveyor's stamp
  • ✗No beaconing certificate on plan
  • ✗Demands full payment before any field work
  • ✗Unusually cheap survey fee
  • ✗Name not found on SURCON register
