Quick guide to Area Delineation & Ward Assistant

What this assistant does: It checks whether a coordinate falls inside Prime, Special Prime or General areas and Nigerian wards; converts between WGS84 and the Minna datum (UTM Zone 31N); finds distances to boundaries; and helps share your location.

How to use:

  • Type coordinates: 538156, 787954 (Minna UTM) or 7.15, 3.35 (Lat,Lon).
  • Try natural language: "Which area is 538156, 787954?" or "Convert 7.15, 3.35 to UTM".
  • Ask location: type "my location" to start live tracking, or "where am I now" for a single snapshot.
  • Share location: type "send my location" and follow the chat prompts to send via WhatsApp.
  • Distance to a place: ask "How far is my location to Olumo Rock?"

Privacy & Safety: Geolocation runs in your browser and requires permission. Sharing to WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message — you must press Send. Use the emergency option only for real emergencies.
