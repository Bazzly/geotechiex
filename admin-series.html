Community Series

Put Yourself On The Map

A recurring challenge, posted on GeoTechieX's socials, prompting OTHER surveyors to share their own stories. Each episode is a directive for someone else to respond to, relatable to surveyors and the public alike. Rename the series below any time.

Future Arcs Roadmap

Admin-only, saved in this browser, never touches the public site. Load a draft arc into the builder below to review or fill in its episode prompts (use "Generate the Next Arc with AI" for help), then use "Publish to the Community Series Page" when it's ready to go live.

Publish to the Community Series Page

Turns the series/arc/episodes above into a new entry for the top of assets/js/series-data.js. Paste it in and redeploy to make it the "Current Challenge" on community-series.html — the arc it replaces automatically becomes past series.

Generate the Next Arc with AI

Builds a prompt for a brand-new batch of episodes on any survey-life topic, so the series never runs out of relatable content.

Import an Arc

Paste the JSON the AI gave you. It replaces the arc title, hook, and episode list above.
