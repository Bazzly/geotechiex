🚫

Landlord CANNOT Do This

  • ✗Lock you out without a court order
  • ✗Remove or seize your property
  • ✗Cut off water or electricity to force you out
  • ✗Enter your room without permission
  • ✗Demolish the property while you're inside
  • ✗Demand more than 1 year rent in advance (Lagos)
⚖️

Legal Eviction Process

  1. 1Landlord gives Notice to Quit (correct notice period)
  2. 2Landlord serves 7-day "Intention to Apply for Possession" notice
  3. 3Landlord files case at Magistrates Court
  4. 4Court hearing — you have the right to defend yourself
  5. 5Only after Warrant of Possession can eviction happen
✅

Your Rights as a Tenant

  • ✓Right to receive a receipt for every rent payment
  • ✓Right to quiet enjoyment of the property
  • ✓Right to proper notice before eviction
  • ✓Right to defend yourself in court
  • ✓Right to a written tenancy agreement
  • ✓Right to a refund of security deposit (minus damages)
💰

Rent Advance Rules

Lagos: Maximum 1 year advance only

Lagos Tenancy Law 2011, Section 4

Landlords demanding 2 or more years of rent in advance are acting illegally in Lagos. You can report them or refuse to pay more than 1 year.

🆘

If Illegally Evicted — Do This

  1. 1.Do NOT voluntarily move all your things out
  2. 2.Document everything (photos, videos, witnesses)
  3. 3.Contact a lawyer or visit the Legal Aid Council
  4. 4.File at Magistrates Court for an emergency injunction
  5. 5.In Lagos, report to LASRRA (Lagos State Rental Register)

📚 Key Nigerian Tenancy Laws

  • • Lagos Tenancy Law 2011 — Applies in Lagos State. Covers rent advance cap, receipts, eviction procedures.
  • • Recovery of Premises Act (Cap R4, LFN 2004) — Federal law on eviction procedure and notice periods.
  • • Landlord and Tenant Law — Various state versions; covers tenancy agreements and obligations.

Laws may vary by state. Always confirm with a qualified Nigerian lawyer for your specific situation.
